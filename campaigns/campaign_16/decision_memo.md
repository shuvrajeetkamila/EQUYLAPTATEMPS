# Campaign 16 — Decision Memo

**Outcome: LEARNING_RATE_SENSITIVE** (frozen precedence; the
Gate E mechanism signature is additionally supported and is
reported as a secondary finding, exactly as preregistered).

**Decisions:**
1. Connector comparisons in this project must henceforth either
   tune the learning rate per connector under a preregistered
   rule or report full LR×budget trajectories. Single-LR,
   single-budget comparisons (the Campaigns 7–15 norm) are
   retired as evidence about connector quality.
2. The residual-identity connector remains the reference design:
   it is the only connector with zero installation damage, and
   at adequate LR/budget it produces the best children measured
   (6.04–6.11). The historical connector's early leads are an
   optimization-scale artifact, not a virtue.
3. Research priority shifts from connectors to **placement**:
   +9.24 of the remaining ≈+3.3–9.2 damage (depending on setting)
   is topology, and no connector or LR recovers it.

**Not authorized:** Campaign 17 execution, PR merge, releases,
held-out evaluation.
