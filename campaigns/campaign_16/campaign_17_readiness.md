# Campaign 16 — Campaign 17 Readiness (report only)

**Readiness verdict: ready for a placement-redesign study; not
ready for a cross-family transfer claim.**

Rationale: the connector question is now understood (damage-free
initialization + LR/budget-aware comparison), but the topology
used since Campaign 13 costs +9.24 before any donor is involved.
A foreign donor tested inside it would be uninterpretable.

**Recommended Campaign 17 (design only):** a native-depth,
function-preserving placement study on the self-control —
- donor sector replaces recipient blocks at matched depth (same
  block count, no duplication, no reordering beyond the swap), so
  the only initial damage is whatever the connector adds (zero
  for the residual-identity connector at matched width);
- entry gate: the Campaign 14 reconstruction test must pass with
  the sector restored (score = native within frozen tolerance);
- then measure the *placement-only* cost of the depth-matched
  swap with own blocks (expected ≈ 0 by construction — verify),
  and only then introduce a foreign sector with intact vs
  scrambled vs random controls under LR-aware budgets.

A cross-family pilot becomes defensible only after that placement
passes its gates. Do not start Campaign 17 from this report.
