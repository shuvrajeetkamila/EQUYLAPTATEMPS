# Campaign 12 — Decision Memo

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Decision:** Classify Campaign 12 as **Outcome C**. Do not
promote. Held-out remains `NOT_RUN_NO_PROMOTION`. Do not begin a
further campaign automatically.

## Why

Campaign 12 was built to give the learned-structure hypothesis its
strongest fair chance: two scrambling granularities, a fresh third
seed, fresh intact runs (no historical reuse), bit-identical
reproduction of Campaign 11's control, and validity gates that all
ten scrambled runs demonstrably passed. Under those conditions the
intact sector's advantage appeared only at seed 42, vanished at
seed 123 (vs the full-tensor control), and reversed at seed 31415
against both controls. The frozen gate requires all seeds against
both controls; it fails decisively. The 96-step panel is likewise
mixed-to-negative. Usefulness gates fail everywhere as well.

## Consequences for the programme

- Across Campaigns 9–12 the consistent story is now: a
  donor-geometry-aware interface makes cross-family block
  transplantation *function*, and pretrained weight *statistics*
  give small, seed-dependent benefits; but no experiment has
  produced a seed-reproducible advantage attributable to the
  donor's learned weight arrangement. Claims of "knowledge
  transfer" for this pairing are not supported and should not be
  made.
- The method itself (scramble controls with exact statistic
  preservation + frozen per-seed gates) proved decisive and cheap;
  it should be the standing evidentiary bar for any future
  transfer claim in this repository.
- If the programme continues, the highest-value next step is a
  *positive control for the method*: a same-family transplant
  (transformer sector into a transformer host) where learned
  arrangement is expected to matter. If even that shows no
  scramble sensitivity, the null is about the surgery regime
  (budgets/interfaces), not the pairing. This is a recommendation
  only; no new campaign is authorized by this memo.
