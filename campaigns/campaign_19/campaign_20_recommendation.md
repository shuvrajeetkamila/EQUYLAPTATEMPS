# Campaign 20 recommendation (single next action)

Do **not** scale this graft up. The one defensible next experiment
is a diagnostic, preregistered study of *why* any sector works
equally well here: test whether the trained connectors are simply
learning to route around the graft (e.g., connector-capacity and
graft-activity dose-response under the same frozen panel), because
until donor structure matters somewhere measurable, larger foreign
pilots would only spend compute re-measuring connector learning.
Requires fresh user authorization and preregistration.
