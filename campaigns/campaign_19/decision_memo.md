# Campaign 19 — Decision memo

**Verdict: `DONOR_ADVANTAGE_NOT_SHOWN` (valid negative).**

In plain terms: we finally ran the real foreign transplant — a
piece of the Mamba model placed inside the SmolLM model, with only
the connecting plugs allowed to learn. The plumbing worked
perfectly. The science did not cooperate: a version with the
donor's weights deliberately shuffled did slightly *better* than
the intact donor in every trial, random weights did about the
same, and switching the graft off changed little. Whatever the
connectors learned, they did not need the donor's knowledge to
learn it.

This is the most valuable kind of negative result: the controls
were strong enough that a positive claim would have meant
something — so the negative means something too. The sealed
held-out set was never opened, because the rules said not to go
looking for a friendlier answer. If this line continues, the next
question is whether the connectors are quietly routing around the
graft; that study is sketched in `campaign_20_recommendation.md`
and needs your authorization like this one did.
