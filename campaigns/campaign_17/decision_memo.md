# Campaign 17 — Decision memo

**Label: `INTERFACE_REPAIRED_STRUCTURAL_DAMAGE_REMAINS`** (frozen
precedence in `decision_rules.md`).

The placement question Campaign 17 was created to answer is settled
affirmatively: the pipeline can preserve the recipient's native
depth, block count, original order, and exact function, with a
self-control (identity replacement) that is bitwise exact — not
approximately exact. The historical structural defect (deleted
blocks 11–12, duplicated sector 24–29, depth 30→34) is removed by
construction in the new engine, which cannot express deletion or
duplication at all.

The same experiment shows why that victory is not yet transfer
readiness: exchanging two *native* sectors' depth positions — same
weights, perfect interfaces, zero tensor changes — costs +8.40 NLL.
Function lives in blocks **at their depths**, not in blocks alone.
Under the frozen readiness rule (D1 ≤ 0.5) Campaign 17 therefore
does **not** authorize claiming readiness; it authorizes designing a
Campaign 18 that first maps structural-exchange damage within-model
(distance/position scaling), then preregisters a cross-family pilot
informed by that map. Connector optimization remains secondary.

No training, no foreign donor, no held-out data was used. PR #1
stays open and unmerged.
