# Campaign 18 readiness (from Campaign 17)

**Status: NOT READY for a naive cross-family pilot; READY to design
an informed one.**

Established by Campaign 17:
- Native-depth placement machinery is exact (bitwise identity and
  reconstruction controls) — placement is no longer a confound.
- Structural exchange of native blocks is substantially damaging and
  distance-sensitive (+0.15 adjacent swap; +8.40 cross-region sector
  exchange), so position/depth placement is a first-order scientific
  variable, not an implementation detail.

A defensible Campaign 18 must therefore:
1. Preregister an exchange-damage map within the recipient (varying
   distance/position under the frozen engine) OR justify skipping it;
2. Choose the foreign graft position/sector using that map, frozen
   before any result;
3. Keep the Campaign 17 identity/reconstruction controls as gates:
   any future child must first pass its self-controls exactly;
4. Keep connectors secondary and report initial damage separately
   from any training recovery, as in Campaigns 14–16.

Opening Campaign 18 requires a new user decision; nothing here
starts it.
